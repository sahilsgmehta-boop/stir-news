Inter-market

The same structure in two markets, A minus B, in basis points of price (an outright's price gap is the rate gap the other way round). Positive and rising = A rich against B. Spreads are front minus back, flies front − 2×middle + back. Both sides can be the same market (e.g. SOFR Dec26-Mar27 vs Jun27-Sep27), each side has its own structure (a 3M spread against a 6M one, a spread against a fly, Euribor 3M against SONIA 6M), and the Ratio bar sets the lots: 2 : 3 shows 2×A − 3×B (default 1:1). Beta hedge sizes B from how the two have moved together; Equal $ risk matches dollars per bp across currencies. The scanners below stay 1:1 with the same structure on both sides. Fed funds pair with SOFR 3M one month on: picking SOFR Dec26-Mar27 on one side puts Fed funds Jan27-Apr27 on the other, the same two meetings (either contract can still be changed by hand). History is each contract's daily close from Eikon over the last 10 months; today's point is live from the board (about 10 minutes delayed).

What works

Hedge ratios

How many lots of one contract match the dollar risk per bp (DV01) of another, at today's FX. Type your lots (negative for short) and pick the contract: every other contract shows the lots that offset it, rounded to the nearest whole lot, and what is left over after rounding. Click a row to pin it in the card below.

MPC SONIA strip against 3M SONIA

A 3M SONIA contract compounds SONIA from its IMM Wednesday to the next one; an MPC SONIA contract compounds it from one Bank of England decision to the day before the next. Both pay £25 per bp of their own rate, so a 1bp move in one MPC period moves a 3M contract by only the share of its days that period covers. The strip is the MPC contracts that cover a 3M contract's three months, each weighted by its days of overlap: that is the hedge for meeting-by-meeting moves, and compounding the MPC rates over those days gives the 3M price the MPC market implies. Basis = 3M price − strip-implied price, in bp: positive = 3M is rich against the strip (sell 3M, buy the strip), negative = cheap. Live prices; rates assumed flat inside each MPC period (no meeting in between).

DV01 per contract

STIR DV01s are set by the contract. Bond futures follow their cheapest-to-deliver bond and change slowly over time: the US figures are Bloomberg's as used in your InTouch report (Oct 2026); German and gilt futures are worked out live from Eikon (the future's price times the duration of a bond like its cheapest-to-deliver, at today's benchmark yield) and fall back to my estimates without it. Live model shows that calculation for every bond future, so the US ones double as a check on Bloomberg's numbers (hover the ⓘ for the inputs). Type your desk's number into any of them; it's saved on this PC.

Commodity ideas

Packages built from the commodity board's energy, grain, oilseed, sugar and livestock calendar structures (spreads, flies, double flies, condors; the expiring front contract is left out): Single a structure on its own · Next structure against the next one of the same kind at 1:1, 1:2 or 2:1 · Curve pair any two nearby structures, ratio fitted then snapped to real lot ratios · Curve, 3 legs a spread against two nearby structures · Year-on-year box the same structure a year later, 1:1 · Cross-product the same structure in two products, by $ value. Year-on-year box / 2-year box / Year-on-year fly the same structure 1 or 2 years later (season cancels) · Strip spread / Strip box calendar-year, quarter, winter and summer strips across years · Crack & arb inter-commodity spreads by month, with their spreads and flies: gasoil, heating oil, RBOB and 3-2-1 cracks, WTI−Brent, RBOB−HO, HO−gasoil, TTF−NBP, TTF−Henry Hub, soyoil−HO, wheat−corn, Kansas−Chicago and Matif−Chicago wheat, the soy crush and the white sugar premium · Crack & arb box one of those spreads against the next one or the same one a year later · Cross-product fitted pairs of logically linked products only. Each must pass the same range rules as STIR ideas, and both sides must move (50–200% of each other). The seasonal-neutral families, singles and next structures are judged on the longest of 2 years, 18 months, 1 year or 7 months that holds; the curve pairs on 7 months. Values are in the product's own units ($ per lot across products); $ figures are for 10 lots of the first structure. Seasonal spreads (gas winter/summer, corn old/new crop) can look like ranges - check earlier years before trading them.

How this scan works

A scan of tens of thousands of packages built from 10 months of Eikon closes, contracts MAR27–DEC29 (DEC28 for CORRA, SARON and €STR), from 3/6/9/12M spreads, 3/6/12M flies, 3M double flies and outrights: Curve – one market against itself, any two structures (4 lots of a 3M spread against 1 of a 12M, a spread against a fly…); Curve, 3 legs – a spread against two other structures nearby; Inter-market – the same structure in two markets, 1 : 1 by DV01, for the logical pairs and, separately, every other pair; Cross-market box – (A − B) on one structure against (A − B) on another; SOFR vs Fed funds, meeting spreads included; and 3-market flies – one market against two others on the same contract, outrights (ranked first) and 3M / 6M spreads only. Each package is the first leg minus a hedge in a plain lot ratio (see below). It is kept only if what is left over has stayed in a range for the last 7 months – it hasn't left the range set before the last two weeks, hasn't made a new 10-month high or low, and the range hasn't drifted (both halves sit at the same level) – and keeps coming back to its mean (a statistical mean-reversion test, a half-life of 2–40 days, 93%+ of days within ±2σ, and enough movement to trade). Survivors are ranked by how stretched they are now; In its range shows where today sits, low (left) to high (right). Markets: click a market once to require it (✓), again to exclude it (✕), a third time to clear; with ✓ markets picked, choose whether ideas must include them or use only them. Logical only keeps single-market ideas and those whose markets are all logical pairs of each other. Signal says which way: Sell = the package is rich. 🎯 Preferred trades are the ones shaped the way you like to trade them: the structure has stayed inside a horizontal range for the whole 7 months (neither edge broken by more than a brief poke), has tested the edge it is at now at least twice before (separate swings), hasn't broken out in the last week, and is back at that edge – the red lines on the chart are the range edges. Stretch is ignored there; the other filters apply. Inter-market (exact) – the identical structure in two markets, same contracts and spacing (SOFR MAR28-JUN28 against Euribor MAR28-JUN28, SONIA JUN28 6M fly against Euribor JUN28 6M fly): outrights, 3/6/9/12M spreads, 3/6/12M flies, 3M double flies and 3/6M condors, 1 : 1 by DV01. Govt families use government bond yields (2Y/5Y/10Y/30Y for the US, Germany, UK, Canada and Japan, from the Curves page feed): the range is judged on 5, 3, 2 or 1 years or 7 months – the longest window that holds is used (half-life up to a sixth of the window); the same yield, curve (e.g. 2s10s) or fly (e.g. 2s5s10s = 2 × 5Y − 2Y − 10Y) in two countries, 1 : 1 in yield bp, with the bonds to buy and sell and futures where listed (TU/FV/TY/US, Schatz/Bobl/Bund/Buxl, long gilt). Ratios are lot ratios only: within one market a fitted ratio snaps to one a trader would use (1:4, 1:3, 1:2, 2:3, 1:1, 3:2, 2:1, 5:2, 3:1, 4:1, 5:1, 6:1), and a leg that would need less than 0.2 is dropped. Across markets there is no fitted multiplier: the same structure in each market (a 6M spread against a 6M spread, a fly against a fly), 1 : 1 by DV01 – the same dollar risk per bp on each side – and 3-market flies are the middle against half of each wing. Both sides have to move: an idea is dropped if the hedge side (as traded) moves less than half as much as the first leg, or more than twice as much, day to day or over the window – otherwise it is really one leg on its own, not a pair – and in a three-leg package every leg must move at least a quarter as much. Hedge is information only: how closely the legs' daily moves follow each other and how steady the ratio has been. These are leads to check, not recommendations: the ratios are fitted on the same history they are tested on, and back months can be thin.
